# Instagram 720 is the daily SKU — Fast 20 must complete

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Product:** VaryForge  
**Depends on:** live Fast 20 timing (`docs/ops/fast-20-timing-2026-08-25.md`)

## Symptom

Testers are about to get the tool. Instagram downloads are **720p**. A live
Fast 20 of a 22s 720 talking-head (`portrait.mp4`) hit
`executionTimeout` at 60 minutes: 8 copies `done` on-worker after **six
encodes each**, 0 returned. Telling them “stay at 8” or “upload 1080” is
not a product. TikFusion does not do that. We will not either.

Crop-only SSIM on that clip:

| Crop | Bits vs source |
|---|---|
| keep 0.94, y 0.50 (live caption-safe center) | **20** — below the 24-bit gate |
| keep 0.92, y 1.00 (punch from top) | 24 |
| keep 0.86–0.90, y 1.00 | **25–26** |

The centered 4–8% punch was right for a **1080** pack that already had
uniqueness headroom and was eating a word (`ced7cbec7c49`). On Instagram
720 it sits under the gate, so autotune bisected five times, then
escalated — 25 minutes for 8, timeout for 20.

## What we will not do

- Raise `TARGET_BITS` / `MIN_PEER_BITS` (stay **24 / 24**)
- Face-zoom crop `0.72` / `0.78`
- Pixel AI scramble / stacked 720 chroma snow
- Tell operators they must source 1080
- PATCH live Fast from this doc (lab pin, Jeff signs look, then live)

## Change

1. **Fail-forward Fast uniqueness.** One medium encode, then the existing
   strong escalate. `FAST_TUNE_MAX_ITERS = 1`. Worst case 2 encodes per
   copy, not 6. A 20-pack is 3 waves of 8 on `jobs=8` — it can finish
   inside 3600s.
2. **Skip peer SSIM on talking-head.** Peer gate was already off (still
   face lands ~13 bits). We were still paying 8×8 ffmpeg SSIM on wave 2
   (~160s uniqueness dwell). Record `min_bits_vs_peers=None`.
3. **Do not VMAF a medium encode we are about to discard.** Quality-gate
   the keeper only.
4. **Instagram 720 crop.** Punch leftover from the **top**
   (`crop_y_frac` 0.90–1.00) so burned-in words stay. Talking-head keep
   remaps to **0.86–0.90** medium / **0.82–0.88** strong (above 0.78).
   1080 keeps the signed 0.92–0.96 / y 0.35–0.65 band.

Gate stays 24. Fast still never face-protects. Color stays zero-mean.
`sample` stays pure.
